Research

Methods that fit the problem

Epidemiological data arrive late, unevenly and with many zeros. My dissertation modeled dengue counts in Pernambuco with zero-inflated models and panel data. My doctoral work extends that line toward decision support, with a sandwich period at the University of Exeter.

I try to recommend the method that suits the question, not the one I already know. That means comparing families of models, including count regression, multicriteria analysis, causal inference and machine learning, and being honest about where each one fits.

Epidemiological surveillance Dengue, decision support systems, news monitoring for early alerting.
Multicriteria decision-making Value-Focused Thinking, FITradeoff, PROMETHEE-ROC.
Statistics and causal inference Zero-inflated and panel models, difference-in-differences.
Operations research and ML Mathematical programming, simulation, supervised and unsupervised learning.
