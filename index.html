Eduardo da Silva

PhD Candidate in Production Engineering, Universidade Federal de Pernambuco (UFPE)

I develop data-driven decision support systems that combine epidemiological surveillance, multicriteria decision-making, statistical modeling and machine learning. My current work focuses on dengue surveillance and public health decisions in Brazil.

About

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Production Engineering from ASCES-UNITA and a Master's degree in Production Engineering from UFPE, where my dissertation applied zero-inflated models and panel data to dengue cases in Pernambuco. I am now a PhD candidate in the same program, with a sandwich period at the University of Exeter under the co-supervision of Prof. Theo Economou.

I am a member of the Information Systems and Decision Research Group (GPSID), where I work on decision support in health. I am also completing a specialization in Data Science and Analytics at USP/ESALQ. I was approved in a public competitive examination for University Professor at the Universidade de Pernambuco (UPE), in the area of operations administration, production administration, logistics and technology.

Research Interests

Epidemiological Surveillance

Dengue surveillance, decision support systems for public health, and news monitoring for early alerting.

Multicriteria Decision-Making

Value-Focused Thinking (VFT), FITradeoff and outranking methods such as PROMETHEE-ROC.

Statistical Modeling

Count models, zero-inflated and panel data models, causal inference with difference-in-differences, and regression analysis.

Operations Research

Mathematical programming, simulation, and prescriptive analytics for industrial and public management problems.
