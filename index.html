Selected work

DengueSphere

Decision support system for dengue epidemiological surveillance · Software registered with INPI (2025)

A decision support platform built to help surveillance teams read dengue data and compare alternatives for action. Developed together with Prof. Maisa Mendonça Silva and presented at the 12th International Conference on Decision Support System Technology (ICDSST 2026).

Dengue News Intelligence

News monitoring dashboard · GPSID, UFPE · Software registered with INPI (2026)

A monitoring panel that collects dengue coverage in the news, classifies it with natural language processing and language models, and supports public health intelligence. Applied in the state health secretariat of Pernambuco.

Value-Focused Thinking and FITradeoff for vector control

Multicriteria decision-making · Recife

A combined approach that structures objectives with Value-Focused Thinking and ranks vector control alternatives with the FITradeoff decision support system, to guide environmental surveillance of Aedes aegypti.

Research

Lines of inquiry

Epidemiological surveillance draws on data that arrive late, unevenly and with many zeros. My dissertation modeled dengue counts in Pernambuco with zero-inflated models and panel data, and my doctoral work extends that line toward decision support, with a sandwich period at the University of Exeter.

On the methods side, I work with multicriteria decision analysis (Value-Focused Thinking, FITradeoff, PROMETHEE-ROC), causal inference through difference-in-differences for policy evaluation, and classical and modern supervised learning. I try to recommend the method that fits the problem, not the one I already know.
